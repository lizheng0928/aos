1.4 Library — Your Single Source of Truth for Work

Library is a built-in app in AOS Work that manages all your files — PDFs, images, videos, all kinds of Office documents, and more.

You probably have many files that live in different places — your laptop, SharePoint or other systems — places the Agent cannot access. Once uploaded to Library, they become readable and accessible to the Agent, turning into important work context that the Agent can read, reference, and work on.

Library needs a one-time authorization in Explore.

Here's how:

1.

Open Explore → Apps — Click Explore in the left sidebar, then select the Apps tab. You will see both Library and Base listed.

2.

Enable Library and click Authorize — Click on the Library app card to open its detail page. Toggle the Enabled switch on, then click the Authorize button.

3.

Complete OAuth Authorization — A pop-up window will appear asking you to log in to your BasilAOS account and grant permissions. Click "Allow" or "Authorize" to complete the process.

4.

Verify Connection — Once authorized, Library will show "Enabled" status.

You can now access Library from the left sidebar anytime — one click away.

What's in Library:

•

My Space — your personal file storage

•

Team Space — shared team folders

•

Recent / Shared with Me / Favorites — quick access to what you need

•

Search — find any file without digging through folders

How to Use Library

Upload Files

Click the Upload button in Library. Choose to upload to My Space (personal) or Team Space (shared).

You can upload files or entire folders in batch.

Find Files

Use the search box in Library to search by name, tag, or content.

Or click + in the task input box, select the Library app, and simply type the file name or page content keywords, then the Agent will search for you.
