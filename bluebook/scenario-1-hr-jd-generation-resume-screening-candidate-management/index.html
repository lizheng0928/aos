Whether you're a recruiter or an HRBP, the hiring flow is the same:

1.Write JDCopy from old JDs, reformat for each channelMultilingual, multi-format adaptation takes time
2.Screen resumesOpen each one, score manuallyLow efficiency at volume, easy to miss candidates

With AOS Work, these three steps happen in one platform. Data flows automatically. No tool switching.

Sub-scenario A: JD Generation — One Role, Multiple Languages & Formats in One Click

Pain Point

"One role needs to go on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and our website. Each channel has a different format. Plus it needs Italian and English versions. Every time I spend half an hour reformatting."

Steps

  • Prepare the role info Tell the AI the following elements:

ElementDescriptionExample
Role nameIn English and local languageSenior Merchandiser
LevelJunior / Mid / SeniorSenior
Core responsibilities3-5 itemsMerchandise planning, SKU analysis, seasonal buying plan
RequirementsHard requirements5+ years fashion industry, fluent Italian and English
Nice-to-havesPreferred conditionsLuxury brand experience, SAP knowledge
  • Specify output requirements
  • Languages: Italian + English
  • Channel formats: LinkedIn / Indeed / Glassdoor / Company website
  • Special requirements: Exclude discriminatory language, generate formal and social-media versions
  • AI generates the JDs The AI creates multiple versions based on your input.

  • Review and write to the Base After confirming, the AI writes the JDs to the table for easy management and publishing.

Task Instruction

Generate Italian + English JDs for the following role:•Role: Senior Merchandiser•Level: Senior•Core responsibilities: Merchandise planning, SKU four-quadrant analysis, seasonal buying plan developmentRequirements: 5+ years fashion industry, fluent Italian and English, proficient in data analysis tools•Nice-to-haves: Luxury brand experience, SAP knowledgeOutput requirements:1.Adapt for LinkedIn / Indeed / Glassdoor / Company website — four channel formats2.Exclude discriminatory language (age/gender/race/religion/marital status, compliant with EU Anti-Discrimination Directive 2000/78/EC)3.Generate formal and social-media short versions4.Write all versions to the Base

Expected Results

  • 2 languages × 4 channels (LinkedIn / Indeed / Glassdoor / Website) × 2 versions = 16 JDs
  • All written to the Base, organized by channel and language
  • Ready to copy and publish directly from the table
PS: When generating JDs for multiple roles at once, start with one role, confirm quality, then batch the rest. Batch results are written to the same table.

Sub-scenario B: Resume Screening & Matching — 100 Resumes, Ranked in Minutes

Pain Point

"200 resumes for one role. If I spend 5 minutes on each, that's 16 hours of screening. And I still miss great candidates."

Steps

  • Upload resumes Upload a batch of resumes (PDF/Word) to the AOS Work conversation.

  • AI auto-parses The AI reads each resume and extracts key info:

FieldDescription
NameFull name
ExperienceTotal years of work
SkillsIndustry tools, professional skills, software
LanguagesLanguages and proficiency
Last employerMost recent company
Current roleMost recent job title
  • Input job requirements, AI scores the match Tell the AI which role you're hiring for. It calculates a match score (0-100) for each candidate and ranks them.

  • Results written to the Base The match ranking table goes into the table. Hiring managers can view, filter, and sort. Original resume files (PDF/Word) can also be linked to the corresponding records.

  • Natural language follow-up queries You can filter candidates directly with natural language:

List all candidates with 3+ years luxury experience, Italian B2 or above, currently in Milan. Sort by match score descending.

Task Instruction

Parse the following resumes, extract key info (name, experience years, skills, languages, education, current role). Match against the following job requirements:•Role: Senior MerchandiserRequirements: 5+ years fashion industry, Italian native or C1+, English B2+, proficient in Excel and data analysis toolsGenerate a match ranking table (0-100 points), write to the Base, including: Name, Experience years, Current role, Last employer, Skills, Languages, Match score, Rank. Link original resume files to the corresponding records.

Expected Results

  • All resumes auto-parsed, match scores clear at a glance
  • Base supports filtering and sorting — hiring managers can view directly
  • You can query specific candidates using natural language

Advanced: Auto-update Interview Feedback

After the interview, type your evaluation directly in AOS Work:

Candidate Marco Rossi interview feedback: Strong industry experience, deep understanding of merchandising, fluent English, Italian needs improvement. Result: proceeding to second round. Recommend scheduling a technical interview with the team lead.

The AI auto-updates the feedback and result in the Base.

Advanced: Auto-reminder for Candidates Without Follow-up

Set up a scheduled reminder: candidates not followed up within 7 days are auto-flagged :

Set up reminder: In the Base, candidates with status "Pending" and no update for 7+ days should be auto-flagged in red for the recruiter.
PS: Under GDPR and EU Anti-Discrimination Directive (2000/78/EC), personal data in resumes is sensitive. Ensure:1)Only collect information directly relevant to the role;2)Do not use age, gender, race, religion, or marital status as scoring criteria;3)Retain candidate data only as long as needed for the hiring purpose (recommend deleting un-hired candidate data within 6 months).
