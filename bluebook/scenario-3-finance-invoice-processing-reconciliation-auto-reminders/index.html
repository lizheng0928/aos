Chapter 3 · Role-Specific Scenarios

3.3 Finance: Invoice Processing, Reconciliation & Reporting

Finance work revolves around "numbers" and "vouchers" — processing hundreds of invoices a month, reconciling with suppliers, tracking payment progress. Repetitive work with very little room for error. This chapter shows you how to automate the mechanical work: batch invoice recognition with auto-entry, reconciliation across systems in one task, and auto-reminders for overdue payments.

The Finance Workflow

Whether you're in accounts payable or financial planning, the daily finance work follows similar logic:

Step

Traditional approach

Pain point

1. Invoice processing

Manually extract invoices from email/system, enter one by one

Hundreds of invoices a month, time-consuming and error-prone

2. Reconciliation

Compare supplier invoices against internal records in Excel

Hours of work at high volume, hard to locate discrepancies

3. Payment tracking

Manual check of due invoices, manual payment scheduling

Easy to miss due invoices, affecting supplier relationships

4. Report generation

Manual data aggregation, financial report creation

Scattered data sources, slow aggregation

With AOS Work, these four steps happen in one platform: AI auto-recognizes invoices, batch-reconciles, monitors due dates, and generates reports. Finance only needs to review and confirm.

Sub-scenario A: Batch Invoice Processing — Hundreds of Invoices, Auto-Recognized & Auto-Entered

Pain Point

"Every month, 200–300 invoices come in from supplier emails. Each is a different PDF format. I have to manually extract supplier name, invoice number, amount, date, tax — and enter them into the system. This alone takes two days."

Steps

1.

Upload the invoice files. Upload a batch of invoices (PDF/scans) to the AOS Work conversation, or extract attachments directly from your email (if you've connected your email Connector). Different suppliers, different formats — all mixed together.

2.

AI auto-recognizes and extracts. The AI reads each invoice and extracts key fields:

Field

Description

Supplier name

Billing company name

Invoice number

Unique ID

Invoice date

Date of issue

Due date

Payment deadline

Net amount

Amount before tax

Tax amount

VAT / IVA amount

Gross amount

Total amount

Currency

EUR / USD / GBP etc.

PO number

Purchase order number (if available)

3.

AI auto-validates. The AI checks data consistency:

Validation

Description

Amount calculation

Net + Tax = Gross

Tax rate check

Does the tax rate match local VAT (e.g. Italy standard 22%)

Duplicate detection

Same supplier, same amount, similar dates

PO matching

Does the invoice amount match the PO

4.

Flag anomalies for human review. Valid invoices are auto-entered; anomalies (calculation errors, suspected duplicates, PO mismatches) are flagged for you to confirm.

5.

Write to the Base. All invoice data is written to the table, organized by supplier, date, and status. Invoice originals are stored in Library and linked to the records.

Task Instruction

Batch process the following invoice files (PDF), extract key fields: - Supplier Name, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Due Date, Net Amount, Tax Amount, Gross Amount, Currency, PO Number Automated validation: 1. Verify amount calculation (Net + Tax = Gross) 2. Verify tax rate compliance with Italian VAT standard rate (22%) or reduced rates 3. Detect duplicate invoices (same supplier, same amount, similar dates) 4. PO number matching check Output Requirements: 1. Auto-write validated invoices to Base 2. Flag anomalous invoices separately, list exception reasons 3. Store invoice originals in Library and link to corresponding records 4. Generate processing summary: total invoices, passed count, exception count

Expected Results

•

All invoices auto-recognized, extracted, and validated in one pass

•

Valid invoices auto-entered, anomalies flagged separately

•

Processing summary: total, passed, exceptions

•

Invoice originals auto-archived, traceable anytime

Advanced: Invoice Data Summary & Visualization

After entry, use natural language to analyze:

Summarize this month's total invoice amount by supplier, sort descending, and create a Top 10 supplier pie chart.

List all due-but-unpaid invoices, sort by due date ascending, flag overdue days.

Compliance note: Under EU e-Invoicing Directive (2014/55/EU) and Italy's SDI (Sistema di Interscambio), electronic invoices must meet specific format standards. AI-extracted data is for assisted entry. Final financial confirmation and payment approval must be done by the right people.

Summary

In short: Agent handles invoice processing and reconciliation; you focus on financial decisions.
