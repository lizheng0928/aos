Chapter 3 · Role-Specific Scenarios

Scenario 3 · Finance: Invoice Processing, Reconciliation & Auto-Reminders

Finance work revolves around "numbers" and "vouchers" — processing hundreds of invoices a month, reconciling with suppliers, tracking payment progress. Repetitive work with very little room for error. This chapter shows you how to automate the mechanical work: batch invoice recognition with auto-entry, multi-system reconciliation in one click, and auto-reminders for overdue payments.

The Finance Workflow

Whether you're in AP or FP&A, the daily finance work follows the same logic:

StepTraditional approachPain point
1.Invoice processingManually extract invoices from email/system, enter one by oneHundreds of invoices a month, time-consuming and error-prone
2.ReconciliationCompare supplier invoices against internal records in ExcelHours of work at high volume, hard to locate discrepancies
3.Payment trackingManual check of due invoices, manual payment schedulingEasy to miss due invoices, affecting supplier relationships
4.Report generationManual data aggregation, financial report creationScattered data sources, slow aggregation

With AOS Work, these four steps happen in one platform: AI auto-recognizes invoices, batch-reconciles, monitors due dates, and generates reports. Finance only needs to review and confirm.

Sub-scenario A: Batch Invoice Processing — Hundreds of Invoices, Auto-Recognized & Auto-Entered

Pain Point

"Every month, 200-300 invoices come in from supplier emails. Each is a different PDF format. I have to manually extract supplier name, invoice number, amount, date, tax — and enter them into the system. This alone takes two days."

Steps

  • Upload the invoice files Upload a batch of invoices (PDF/scans) to the AOS Work conversation. Different suppliers, different formats — all mixed together.

  • AI auto-recognizes and extracts The AI reads each invoice and extracts key fields:

FieldDescription
Supplier nameBilling company name
Invoice numberUnique ID
Invoice dateDate of issue
Due datePayment deadline
Net amountAmount before tax
Tax amountVAT / IVA amount
Gross amountTotal amount
CurrencyEUR / USD / GBP etc.
PO numberPurchase order number (if available)
  • AI auto-validates The AI checks data consistency:

ValidationDescription
Amount calculationNet + Tax = Gross
Tax rate checkDoes the tax rate match local VAT (e.g. Italy standard 22%)
Duplicate detectionSame supplier, same amount, similar dates
PO matchingDoes the invoice amount match the PO
  • Flag anomalies for human review Valid invoices are auto-entered; anomalies (calculation errors, suspected duplicates, PO mismatches) are flagged for you to confirm.

  • Write to the Base All invoice data is written to the table, organized by supplier, date, and status. Invoice originals are stored in Library and linked to the records.

Task Instruction

Batch process the following invoice files (PDF), extract key fields:•Supplier Name, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Due Date, Net Amount, Tax Amount, Gross Amount, Currency, PO NumberAutomated validation:1.Verify amount calculation (Net + Tax = Gross)2.Verify tax rate compliance with Italian VAT standard rate (22%) or reduced rates3.Detect duplicate invoices (same supplier, same amount, similar dates)4.PO number matching checkOutputRequirements:1.Auto-write validated invoices to Base2.Flag anomalous invoices separately, list exception reasons3.Store invoice originals in Library and link to corresponding records4.Generate processing summary: total invoices, passed count, exception count

Expected Results

  • All invoices auto-recognized, extracted, and validated in one pass
  • Valid invoices auto-entered, anomalies flagged separately
  • Processing summary: total, passed, exceptions
  • Invoice originals auto-archived, traceable anytime

Advanced: Invoice Data Summary & Visualization

After entry, use natural language to analyze:

Summarize this month's total invoice amount by supplier, sort descending, and create a Top 10 supplier pie chart.
List all due-but-unpaid invoices, sort by due date ascending, flag overdue days.
PS: Under EU e-Invoicing Directive (2014/55/EU) and Italy's SDI (Sistema di Interscambio), electronic invoices must meet specific format standards. AI-extracted data is for assisted entry. Final financial confirmation and payment approval must be done by authorized personnel.

Summary

The core value of finance is data accuracy and compliance, not manual invoice entry. Let the Agent handle invoice recognition and data aggregation. Finance professionals spend their time on what needs real judgment: reconciliation exceptions and budget analysis.

Recommended combination:

ScenarioRecommended SkillDescription
Data summaryData Analysis SkillAuto-summarize and analyze cleaned data
Reconciliation reminderScheduled TaskPeriodic account-age check, auto-remind when overdue

Next step: Start with invoice recognition. Get invoices into the Base, then have the Agent periodically summarize and generate reconciliation reports. Remember: AI-extracted data needs human review. Final approval is by authorized personnel.
