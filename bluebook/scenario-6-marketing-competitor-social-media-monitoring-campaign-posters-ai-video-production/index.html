Chapter 3 · Role-Specific Scenarios

Scenario 6 · Marketing: Competitor Social Media Monitoring, Campaign Posters & AI Video Production

Marketing runs on "content" and "monitoring" — tracking competitors, creating campaign posters, producing social media videos. Much of the daily work is repetitive and time-consuming. This chapter shows you how to automate competitor social media monitoring, generate multi-size campaign posters in one click, and create on-brand videos directly from your Library — no need to search for assets, AI pulls from your existing brand library and keeps everything on-brand.

The Marketing Workflow

Whether you're a brand manager, digital marketing specialist, or content creator, the daily flow is the same:

StepTraditional approachPain point
1.Competitor monitoringManually browse Instagram, Facebook, screenshot dataHalf a day to produce one competitor report
2.Campaign postersDesigner creates each size individually, multiple revisionsLong turnaround, 5 sizes = 5 layout adjustments
3.Social media videoHire a supplier, shoot, edit, adapt for multiple platforms2-week delivery, high cost

With AOS Work, these three steps happen in one platform: AI auto-monitors competitor social media, pulls assets from Library to generate multi-size posters and videos in one go. Marketing only reviews and makes the final call.

Sub-scenario A: Competitor Social Media Monitoring — Your 24/7 Competitor Radar

Pain Point

"Every time I do competitor analysis, I manually browse Instagram and Facebook, screenshot data. Half a day for one report."

Steps

  • Set monitoring targets Tell the AI which competitors and dimensions to monitor:

ElementDescriptionExample
Competitor brandsBrands to watchBrand R**
ChannelsSocial platformsInstagram, Facebook
FrequencyHow often to runEvery Monday 9:00 AM auto-execute
Focus dimensionsWhat to look atProduct launches, promotions, KOL collabs, UGC
  • AI auto-captures via Apify The AI calls the Apify API to extract competitor social media data: post content, engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares), and user comments.

  • AI auto-classifies and extracts data The AI categorizes the captured content:

CategoryDescription
Product showcaseNew launches, product close-ups
PromotionsDiscounts, limited-time offers
KOL collaborationInfluencer posts, co-branded content
UGCUser-generated content
Brand storyBrand philosophy, behind-the-scenes
  • Generate competitor social media weekly report The AI aggregates each brand's weekly activity and produces a comparison report:

Report contentDescription
Posting frequency comparisonHow many posts each brand put up this week
Engagement rankingTop 5 highest-engagement posts
Content strategy differencesWhich brand is product-focused, which is brand-story-focused
Weekly trendsNotable industry movements
  • Write to table + push to email All data goes into the table. The report is pushed to your email.

Task Instruction

Set up competitor social media monitoring:•Brands: R, D, G**- Channels: Instagram, Facebook•Frequency: Every Monday 9:00 AM, auto-execute•Focus dimensions: New product launches, promotional activities, KOL collaborations, user-generated content (UGC) Based on this week's captured data, generate a competitor social media weekly report:1.Posting frequency comparison across brands2.Top 5 posts by engagement (likes + comments + shares)3.Content strategy differentiation analysis (which brand is product-focused vs. brand-story-focused)4.Notable trends this week Write to Base and push to email.

Expected Results

  • Competitor social media weekly report arrives every Monday morning — no manual browsing
  • Competitor data accumulates in the table, supporting long-term trend comparison
  • AI auto-classifies and extracts engagement data — no manual sorting
PS: Apify scrapes Instagram and Facebook public posts, but stability is affected by platform anti-scraping measures. Stick to public posts. Under EU Database Directive (96/9/EC), systematic scraping and reuse of public information should not infringe database rights. Use competitor data internally for decision-making; don't publish raw scraped content externally. PS: Under GDPR, social media posts and comments may contain personal data. Only analyze public business content; do not collect or process personal data from individual users (e.g., commenter profiles, private messages). Use insights internally for marketing strategy. Don't publish raw scraped content externally.

Sub-scenario B: Campaign Poster Generation — Pull Assets from Library, Generate 5 Sizes in One Click

Pain Point

"Every campaign poster has to wait for the designer's schedule. One campaign needs Instagram, Facebook, email, and print — 5 different sizes. The designer manually adjusts the layout 5 times."

What Makes This Different

This isn't generating a poster from scratch. It starts from existing brand assets in Library (product images, campaign assets, model photos). AI generates posters based on the brand template and outputs multiple sizes in one batch — no manual size-by-size layout adjustment.

Steps

  • Pull assets from Library In the conversation, specify which assets in Library to use (product images, campaign assets, model photos).

  • Input poster requirements Tell the AI the campaign theme, brand template, and key info:

ElementDescriptionExample
Campaign themeWhat eventSummer Sale 2026
Brand templateVisual styleMinimalist white background + Logo top-left
Key infoMust-show content8/15-8/31 Storewide 30% Off
  • AI generates poster versions The AI generates 3-4 poster versions for you to choose from.

  • Select version, output all sizes in one batch After selection, the AI auto-adapts to all required sizes:

SizeUseResolution
Instagram Square 1:1Instagram posts1080×1080
Instagram Story 9:16Instagram Stories1080×1920
Facebook Landscape 1.91:1Facebook ads1200×628
Email Banner 3:1EDM1800×600
Print A4 PortraitOffline materials2480×3508
  • Save to Library + write to table All versions go to Library, organized by campaign/date/channel.

Task Instruction

Pull this product image and this Campaign asset from Library to generate an event poster:•Event Theme: Summer Sale 2026•Brand Template: Minimalist white background + Logo top-left•Key Info: 8/15-8/31 Storewide 30% Off First generate 3-4 poster versions for selection. After selection, generate all the following size versions in one batch:1.Instagram 1:1 (1080×1080)2.Instagram Story 9:16 (1080×1920)3.Facebook 1.91:1 (1200×628)4.Email Banner 3:1 (1800×600)5.Print A4 (2480×3508) Auto-adapt layout for each size. Store all versions in Library and write to Base.

Expected Results

  • Uses existing Library assets — no design from scratch
  • AI generates 3-4 versions for selection, then outputs 5 sizes in one batch
  • Designer only needs final fine-tuning — no manual size-by-size layout
  • All versions auto-saved to Library for team collaboration
PS: If AI-generated human imagery is used in posters, label it "AI-generated imagery" to comply with EU AI Act transparency requirements. Ensure brand asset copyrights are properly licensed.

Sub-scenario C: AI Video Generation — From Library Assets to Social Media Short Clips (Advanced)

Pain Point

"Creating a social media product video means hiring a supplier, shooting, editing, and adapting for multiple platforms. 2-week delivery, high cost."

What Makes This Different

This doesn't start from scratch finding assets. It uses existing model photos or campaign assets from Library. The AI designs prompts based on the social media context and generates platform-appropriate short videos.

Steps

  • Pull assets from Library Select existing assets: model try-on photos, campaign shots, product white-background images.

  • Choose the social media context and style Tell the AI the platform and style:

Copy pointsMust-include messageNew Release

  • Select version, auto-adapt to multi-platform sizes After selection, the AI auto-adapts to each platform:

PlatformRatioDescription

TikTok9:16Vertical full-screen
YouTube Shorts9:16Vertical full-screen
Facebook1:1Square

Expected Results

  • AI auto-designs prompts, generates video, adapts to multi-platform sizes
