Chapter 3 · Role-Specific Scenarios

Scenario 7 · Sales: Lead Automation & Multi-Source Prospecting

Sales runs on "clients" and "leads" — collecting business cards at trade shows, following up, developing new retail accounts. Every task needs quick response and data support. This chapter shows you how to turn business card photos into leads instantly, auto-research customer backgrounds, intelligently score and rank, and proactively prospect from multiple data sources — so sales teams spend their time closing deals.

The Sales Workflow

Whether you're a sales manager, account manager, or business developer, the daily flow is the same:

StepTraditional approachPain point
1. Trade show lead captureCollect business cards, organize them at homeLeads go cold by the time you get home, no context for follow-up
2. Customer researchManually check Google, LinkedIn, Instagram4-5 platforms per customer, time-consuming
3. Find new retail accountsWalk the streets, rely on network, guessNo data on which neighborhoods have target stores
4. Follow-up managementExcel tracking, easy to forgetLeads go cold after a week without contact

With Workbench, these four steps happen in one platform: snap a business card photo → instant structured lead, AI auto-researches customer background from multiple sources, Apify proactively searches for target stores, follow-up status is auto-monitored.

Sub-scenario A: Customer Profile & Lead Automation — From Business Card Photo to Follow-up Suggestions

Pain Point

"Collect a stack of business cards at the trade show. By the time I organize them at home, leads are cold. Customer background research is manual. No context for follow-up."

What Makes This Valuable

Snap a business card photo at the trade show → upload → AI parses and writes to the table → auto-researches customer background (Google Maps / LinkedIn / Instagram / website) → AI gives partnership recommendations. From lead capture to follow-up, all in one step. Leads don't go cold.

Steps

  • Snap and upload the business card At the trade show, snap a photo and upload it to the Workbench conversation.
  • AI auto-OCR + write to table The AI auto-recognizes the card info:
FieldDescription
CompanyCompany name on the card
ContactName
TitleJob title
PhoneContact number
EmailEmail address
AddressCompany address
  • AI auto-researches customer background The AI automatically researches from multiple channels and creates a complete profile:
ChannelResearch content
Google MapsLocation, business status, rating, review count
LinkedInCompany size, industry, recent updates, key decision-makers
InstagramFollower count, partner brands, content style, recent activity
WebsiteMain business, target customer segment, price range
  • AI gives partnership recommendations You input your follow-up rules. The AI gives recommendations based on the customer profile:
Recommendation dimensionDescription
Match scoreHigh / Medium / Low
Partnership approachWholesale / Retail / E-commerce
First-contact talking pointsBased on the customer's recent activity
Recommended productsBased on the customer's segment and price range
  • Set follow-up reminders Leads not followed up within 7 days are auto-flagged and pushed to Teams.

Task Instruction

Plain Text Recognize this business card's information (Company / Contact Person / Title / Phone / Email / Address), write to multi-dimensional table customer lead tracker, set next follow-up date to 3 days later. Then research this customer's background information: 1. Google Maps: company location, business status, rating and reviews 2. LinkedIn: company size, industry, recent updates 3. Instagram: follower count, partner brands, content style 4. Official website: main business, target customer segment, price range Summarize and write to multi-dimensional table customer profile. Based on the customer profile and my follow-up rules (target customer = chain store + monthly sales 500+ + has Instagram operations), provide partnership advancement recommendations: 1. Match score 2. Recommended partnership approach (wholesale / retail / e-commerce) 3. Suggested first-contact talking points (based on customer's recent activities) 4. Recommended products (based on customer's segment and price range) Set reminder: leads not followed up within 7 days auto-flagged in red and pushed to Teams.

Expected Results

  • Business card photo → structured lead in seconds
  • AI auto-researches 4 channels, generates a complete customer profile
  • AI gives partnership recommendations based on your rules
  • Follow-up status auto-monitored — leads don't go cold

Advanced: Batch Process All Trade Show Business Cards

Collected 50 business cards at the trade show? Snap all photos, upload in batch. The AI processes: recognize → research → score → rank by match. You start following up with high-match leads by the time you get to the hotel.

PS: Personal information on business cards is "personal data" under GDPR. Ensure: 1) You obtained consent when collecting the card for business contact purposes; 2) Card data is used only for B2B business follow-up; 3) Delete unconverted lead data after a reasonable retention period (recommend 12 months).

Pain Point

"Finding new retail accounts means walking the streets or relying on your network. No data on which neighborhoods have target stores. Customer screening lacks data support."

How This Is Different from Scenario A

ComparisonSub-scenario A: Trade Show Lead AutomationSub-scenario B: Multi-Source Prospecting
Customer sourceBusiness cards from trade showsYou proactively search
Search methodExisting contactsSearch by area + conditions
Data sourcesOCR + 4-channel researchGoogle Maps + Instagram + online content
Best forImmediate follow-up after trade shows/eventsExpanding into new markets, developing new clients

Steps

  • Set the target store profile + search area Tell the AI what kind of store you're looking for and where:
ElementDescriptionExample
City/neighborhoodWhere to searchLondon Shoreditch
Store typeWhat kindIndependent fashion boutique
Price rangeWhat tierMid-to-high end
Target customerWho they serve25-35 age group
  • AI searches Google Maps via Apify The AI searches by area + type, returns: store name, address, rating, review count, business status, category.
  • AI multi-source deep research For each candidate store, multi-dimensional research:
ChannelResearch content
Google MapsLocation, street view, rating, review content (analyze customer profile)
Nearby demographic profilePopulation density, spending level, age distribution
LinkedInCompany size, founding year, operating history (chain vs. single store)
InstagramFollower count, partner brands, content style (brand alignment)
Online published contentMulti-source search for the store's content (selection style, existing suppliers)
  • AI match score + partnership recommendations Based on your partnership rules, the AI evaluates each store:
Recommendation dimensionDescription
Match score0-100, weighted across dimensions
Partnership approachWholesale / Consignment / Co-brand / Pop-up
StrengthsWhy this store is a good fit for us
Risk alertsExisting competitor partnership? Mismatched customer base? Unstable operations?
First-contact pitchBased on the store's recent activity and selection style
  • Sort by match score, write to table The AI sorts by match score descending. You start with the Top 5.

Task Instruction

Plain Text Use Apify to call Google Maps API, search for independent fashion boutiques within 2km radius of London Shoreditch district. Return: store name, address, rating, review count, business status, category. Write to multi-dimensional table as candidate store list. Conduct multi-source data research on each candidate store: 1. Google Maps: location, street view, rating, review content, analyze in-store customer profile 2. Nearby area demographic profile (population density, consumption level, age distribution) 3. LinkedIn: company size, founding year, operating history (chain vs. single store) 4. Instagram: follower count, partner brands, content style 5. Multi-source search for store's online published content, understand selection style and existing suppliers Summarize and write to multi-dimensional table store profile. Based on the store profile and my partnership rules (target = independent boutique + mid-to-high end + Instagram followers 5000+ + no direct competitor partnership), provide for each candidate store: 1. Match score (0-100 points) 2. Partnership approach (wholesale / consignment / co-brand / pop-up) 3. Strengths analysis 4. Risk alerts 5. Suggested first-contact pitch Sort by match score from highest to lowest.

Expected Results

  • Auto-search for target stores by area — no more walking the streets
  • Each store has a complete profile (5-channel data aggregated)
  • AI scores and sorts by your rules — start with Top 5
  • Includes risk alerts and partnership approach suggestions — first contact has context

Advanced: Batch Search Multiple Neighborhoods

text Batch search for independent fashion boutiques in London neighborhoods: Shoreditch, Notting Hill, Covent Garden. Aggregate rankings, output a city-level partnership opportunity list.
PS: Under GDPR, collecting B2B contact information from public sources (Google Maps/Instagram) falls under "Legitimate Interest" processing. When first contacting a store, inform them of the data source and provide an opt-out option (Right to Object). Do not use the collected personal information for non-B2B purposes.
