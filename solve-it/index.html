What happens after you submit

1

Understand

How you work now, where you are stuck, what inputs you have, and what result you want.

2

Assess

Map whether Workbench fits the scenario, and identify the Skills, permissions, and delivery route.

3

Validate

Confirm missing details and make sure the proposed approach matches the real work context.

4

Co-Create

Turn high-value scenarios into documented solutions that can be reused by the community.

How to describe your problem effectively

  • Check: What role or task are you in when the problem occurs?
  • Check: What steps do you take today, and how long does it take?
  • Check: What systems, files, tables, emails, or assets are involved?
  • Check: What should Workbench ultimately deliver?
  • Check: Which data, account, or publishing boundaries should stay protected?
